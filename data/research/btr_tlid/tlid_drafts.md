## Draft patterns (lineup-only data, 35 games, TLID picks = 175)

### Takeaway
Within the 35 games found, TLID's most-picked hero is Fanny (17 games), then Marcel (13), Atlas (10) and Rafaela (10). Since 18 Oct, TLID has locked Fanny and Esmeralda in all 5 games found. TLID's opponents most often picked Freya (27), Hirara (17) and Atlas (13). Ban counts for or against TLID, side tendencies and first-pick heroes cannot be computed.

### Cited Findings
All counts below are computed by me from the per-game lineups on [MLBBHub drafts](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/drafts); they cover only the 35 games with lineups (31 regular season + 4 playoff).
- TLID record in games with a recorded winner among these 35: 22 wins (excluding the 8 games in the three conflicting series, see above). Treat as approximate.
- TLID hero picks (number of games the hero appears in TLID's lineup): Fanny 17, Marcel 13, Atlas 10, Rafaela 10, Esmeralda 9, Paquito 8, Mathilda 7, Eudora 7, Obsidia 7, Aulus 7, Novaria 6, Belerick 5, Masha 5. Then 4 each: Uranus, Selena, Moskov, Nolan, Barats, Lukas. 3 each: Melissa, Brody, Kimmy, Guinevere, Carmilla, Alice.
- Fanny by period: Aug 29 vs ONIC (3 games), Aug 30 vs AE (2), Sep 12 and Sep 19 vs BTR (2 each), Oct 4 vs AE (2), Oct 9 vs RRQ (1), Oct 18 vs ONIC (1), 5 Nov vs BTR (4); total 17.
- Fanny + Esmeralda appear together in all 5 games from Oct 18 and 5 Nov (ONIC G1, BTR G1-G4); Fanny + Esmeralda are TLID's late-season core.
- In the 4 playoff games vs BTR, TLID's lineup always included Fanny and Esmeralda; the other three heroes were Eudora/Kalea/Aulus (G1, win), Eudora/Chou/Alice (G2, win), Masha/Lylia/Selena (G3, loss), Paquito/Alice/Harley (G4, win).
- Heroes TLID picked in most of its recorded wins (counting only games with a clean winner label): Fanny 13, Atlas 7, Esmeralda 7, Marcel 7, Eudora 6, Mathilda 5, Aulus 5, Paquito 4.
- Opponents' picks against TLID (games): Freya 27, Hirara 17, Atlas 13, Zhuxin 10, Uranus 10, Lylia 9, Melissa 8, Selena 6, Paquito 6.
- Season-wide, Freya is the most-banned hero (141 bans in 190 drafted games), and blue side won 58% of games. These are league figures. — [MLBBHub drafts](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/drafts)
- Only one Freya pick by TLID in 35 games, while opponents picked her 27 times against TLID.

### Inferences
- A possible phase shift: early/mid season TLID used Marcel, Rafaela, Novaria, Atlas; in Sep-Oct Mathilda/Obsidia/Aulus/Lukas; since 18 Oct Fanny/Esmeralda. This reads from lineups only and could be affected by patch changes or opponent bans, which are not visible.
- TLID may have avoided Freya because it is the league's most-contested hero (likely banned or taken by opponents), but bans are unavailable so this cannot be tested.

### Gaps

- Player signatures: only two unreconciled scoreboards exist (see conflicts), so none are reported.
