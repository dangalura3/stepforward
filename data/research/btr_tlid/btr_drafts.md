# BTR (Bigetron by Vitality) MPL Indonesia Season 18 drafts

Scope note: MPL ID Season 18 only (August to November 2026). Nothing here is from MPL PH or MPL MY. Today is 2026-11-07. Files: `btr_drafts.csv` (515 rows) and this summary.

## Q1. How many games did BTR play, and for how many do we have a draft?

### Takeaway
BTR played 53 games in Season 18 up to and including the 6 Nov 2026 lower bracket semifinal vs Alter Ego (39 regular season games in 16 series, 9 playoff games in 2 series, 5 games vs AE). I found a **partial** draft for 48 of 53 games, and a **complete** draft (all bans in order, all picks in order, sides) for **0 of 53**. What exists is a list of 5 hero names per team per game for 48 games (very probably the bans, attribution to a team unconfirmed, see Q2), plus a one-game scoreboard of picks for 3 series. The 5 games vs Alter Ego on 6 Nov have no ban data at all.

### Cited Findings
- The team name is "Bigetron by Vitality" (abbreviated BTR; mlbbhub prints "BIGETRON BY VIT") — [MLBBHub drafts](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/drafts), [Cybersport.ru match](https://www.cybersport.ru/matches/mlbb/10096685), [DuniaGames roster](https://duniagames.co.id/amp/discover/article/roster-mpl-id-s18-lengkap-susunan-9-tim-terbaru/en). I did not find "Bigetron Alpha" used for Season 18.
- Regular season: BTR played 16 series, 10 wins and 6 losses, finishing 4th (10-6) behind TLID 12-4, NAVI 12-4, AE 10-6 — [MLBBHub playoffs](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/playoffs); series list in [MLBBHub schedule](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/schedule) and [MLBBHub drafts](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/drafts). The 16 regular season series add up to 39 games.
- Playoffs: Round 1 Match 2 BTR 3-2 EVOS (4 Nov); Round 2 Match 2 TLID 3-1 BTR (5 Nov); Lower Bracket Semifinal BTR 3-2 AE (6 Nov, Bo5); a Lower Bracket Final (Bo7) vs the loser of the Upper Bracket Final was listed for Sat 7 Nov 17:30, and the page header named TLID as BTR's next opponent — [MLBBHub playoffs](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/playoffs), [MLBBHub match 1112](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/match/1112). The 7 Nov match had not been played when the pages were read, so it is outside the dataset.
- 53 games matches the "53 games" played by four BTR players on the MLBBHub stats page (39 + 5 + 4 + 5 = 53) — [MLBBHub stats](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/stats). Known game results: BTR won 28 and lost 25 of the 53 (25 wins in the 48 games with per-game winners, plus 3 wins in the AE series, whose individual game results I do not have).
- The MLBBHub drafts page (data stamped 6 Nov 2026 08:55 UTC, "76 of 76 completed matches") ends at 5 Nov, so the 6 Nov BTR vs AE series is not on it — [MLBBHub drafts](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/drafts).
- The match page for BTR vs AE (match 1112) shows series score AE 2-3 BTR, stage "Lower Bracket Semi Finals", dated Fri 6 Nov 2026, but lists no per-game winners, sides, or bans — [MLBBHub match 1112](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/match/1112).

## Q2. What are the drafts: bans, picks, sides, patch?

### Takeaway
The only per-game draft data I could reach is a pair of 5-hero lists per game on the MLBBHub drafts page. They have no "Ban" or "Pick" labels and no sides, and they contain no step order. By testing them against the game 1 scoreboards of two series I concluded they are bans (not picks), but I cannot tell whether a list under team X shows bans made by X or bans made against X. Sides, pick order, ban order and per-game player-hero picks are UNKNOWN for essentially every game.

### Cited Findings
- Each game block on the drafts page contains only the words "Scoreboard", "Game N", "Winner" (before the winning team's list) and the team names; no "Ban", "Pick", "Blue", "Red" or "First Pick" labels. Example of the raw text: "Game 1 NAVI Carmilla Fanny Esmeralda Phoveus Lukas BTR Winner Masha Zhuxin Paquito Gloo Kalea" — [MLBBHub drafts](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/drafts). The page's season stats include "Most Banned" (Freya 141 of 190 games), "Blue side first bans" (Fanny, Atlas) and "Red side first bans" (Freya, Masha) in other sections, and says draft data comes from Liquipedia — same page.
- Why I read the lists as bans: the match pages show a single scoreboard (not tied to a game number) with the heroes actually played. For the BTR vs GEEK series (match 1042) and BTR vs EVOS playoff series (match 1109), in Game 1 no hero on the scoreboard appears in either list, while in every other game of those two series at least one scoreboard hero appears in the lists (for example Uranus in Game 2 and Hayabusa in Game 3 of the GEEK series). A banned hero cannot be played, so if the lists are bans the scoreboard must be Game 1, and the data fit that exactly. If the lists were picks, one game would match the scoreboard fully, and none does — [MLBBHub match 1042](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/match/1042), [MLBBHub match 1109](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/match/1109), [MLBBHub drafts](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/drafts). The hero names in the lists also match the most banned heroes (Freya, Atlas, Fanny, Marcel, Masha, Hirara, Carmilla, Paquito).
- Picks I have (from scoreboards, Game 1 only; player and hero, no order): BTR vs GEEK G1 (16 Aug, BTR won): BTR Shogun Uranus, Nnael Hayabusa, Morenooo Novaria, Finn Mathilda, EMANN Ixia; GEEK MarceL Barats, Nazara Ling, Aboyy Kimmy, AudyTzy Kaja, KennzySkie Brody — [MLBBHub match 1042](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/match/1042). BTR vs EVOS G1 (4 Nov, BTR won): BTR Shogun Yu Zhong, Nnael Nolan, Morenooo Kimmy, Finn Masha, EMANN Esmeralda; EVOS Rendyyy Dyrroth, Alberttt Suyou, Ryzaa Lylia, Muezza Kalea, Erlan Paquito — [MLBBHub match 1109](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/match/1109). BTR vs AE (6 Nov), game number NOT stated on the page and no ban data to check it against: BTR Shogun Uranus, Nnael Nolan, Morenooo Valir, Finn Marcel, EMANN Paquito; AE Nino Barats, Yazukee Suyou, Hijumee Eudora, alekk Khaleed, Arfy Freya — [MLBBHub match 1112](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/match/1112). In the CSV this last block has an empty game_no and winner.
- Patch: the drafts page and match pages show "Patch 2.2.16a" only in the footer; it is not tied to any game, and the patch may have changed during the season, so the patch column in the CSV is empty — [MLBBHub drafts](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/drafts).
- Sides: the page says blue side won 58% (110 of 190 games) and red 42% season wide, but gives no side per game — [MLBBHub drafts](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/drafts). Cybersport.ru and the match pages do not give sides either.

### Inferences
- Reading the lists as bans is well supported (two series fully consistent, other games excluded); which team made them is not. Anyone using these numbers must treat the team label as "list shown under".
- The 48-game ban lists cover the whole regular season and the first two playoff series, but without sides and order they cannot rebuild the 20 step draft.

### Gaps
- Sides (blue/red), ban order, pick order, which team banned each hero: not on the sources I could read. Liquipedia, which has these in its match data, could not be fetched (see below).
- Game 2 to 5 pick/player data for every series, and Game 1 pick/player data for 14 more series: the match pages only show one scoreboard each (Game 1). I opened 3 match pages and stopped, since each further page is one more manual read, and the site forbids automated scraping; reading the remaining 16 pages one at a time would add Game 1 picks only.

## Q3. Roster, roles and changes; sources, blocked sources, conflicts

### Takeaway
BTR's Season 18 five is Shogun (EXP), Nnael (Jungle), Morenooo (Mid), EMANN (Gold), Finn (Roam), the same core that won Season 17, with Miguel as a substitute, coach K Dot, assistant coach Her and technical director Panda. I found no roster change for the season and every scoreboard I opened used the same five.

### Cited Findings
- Roster: EXP Shogun, Jungler Nnael, Mid "Moreno" (written Morenooo on MLBBHub), Gold Emann, Roamer Finn; substitute Miguel; coach K Dot; assistant coach Her; technical director Panda — [DuniaGames S18 roster](https://duniagames.co.id/amp/discover/article/roster-mpl-id-s18-lengkap-susunan-9-tim-terbaru/en). The article reports no new players or transfers and says the core of Shogun, Moreno, Nnael, Emann and Finn is retained from the title winning Season 17 side.
- Lanes in play: MORENOOO MID, EMANN GOLD, FINN ROAM, NNAEL JUNGLE (53 games each), SHOGUN EXP (listed in hero pools) — [MLBBHub stats](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/stats). In the CSV the BTR "role" cell uses these season lanes, not a per-game observation.
- Match scoreboards for 16 Aug, 4 Nov and 6 Nov list Shogun, Nnael, Morenooo, Finn, EMANN — [MLBBHub match 1042](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/match/1042), [MLBBHub match 1109](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/match/1109), [MLBBHub match 1112](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/match/1112). Cybersport.ru's 3 Oct page also lists Shogun, Nnael, Moreno, EMANN, Finn — [Cybersport.ru](https://www.cybersport.ru/matches/mlbb/10096685).
- BTR won MPL ID Season 17 — [DuniaGames roster](https://duniagames.co.id/amp/discover/article/roster-mpl-id-s18-lengkap-susunan-9-tim-terbaru/en) (the article says "last season"); also the search hit [The Game PH](https://thegame.ph/e-sports/bigetron-are-the-mpl-id-season-17-champions/) (title only, not opened).

### Conflicts between sources
- Match dates: the MLBBHub schedule page summary placed BTR vs DEWA on Sun 16 Aug (18:00), BTR vs NAVI on Sun 23 Aug, BTR vs GEEK (second meeting) on Sun 20 Sep and BTR vs AE (second meeting) on Sun 4 Oct. A Gizmologi search result title ("Jadwal MPL ID S18 Hari Ini: BTR vs Dewa United ... 21 Agustus") backs 21 Aug for DEWA, and match numbers run in time order (e.g. 1084 sits between 1082 and 1087), so I used the drafts page dates. Source: [MLBBHub schedule](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/schedule), [MLBBHub drafts](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/drafts), [Gizmologi](https://gizmologi.id/game/jadwal-mpl-id-s18-hari-ini-21-agustus/).

Not resolved. Match 1109 is similar (19:15 scheduled, 19:39 stamped).

- Cybersport.ru shows 3 Oct 13:00 for BTR vs RRQ while MLBBHub shows 17:00 WIB; this is consistent with a time zone difference (not verified).
- The first reading of the drafts page called the hero lists "picks"; the scoreboard test above shows they are bans, so ignore any "picks" label elsewhere in my intermediate reads.

## CSV notes
- Columns exactly: game_id,date,stage,series,game_no,blue_team,red_team,winner,step_no,action_team,side,action,hero,player,role,patch,source_url.
- Ban rows (480 rows = 48 games x 10): `action_team` holds `listed_under:<TEAM>` (the team whose block the hero appeared in on the drafts page), not a confirmed "banned by". `step_no`, `side`, `blue_team`, `red_team`, `patch` are empty because they are unknown. Rows for a game and team keep the page's list order, which may or may not be ban order.
- Pick rows (30 rows, Game 1 of three series): hero and player from the scoreboard; `step_no` empty; `role` filled for BTR players only from season lanes. The 6 Nov block has empty game_no and winner, and game_id `S18-BTR-20261106-AE-GUNCONFIRMED`.
- 5 placeholder rows (one per AE game on 6 Nov) have empty action and hero, meaning draft UNKNOWN.
