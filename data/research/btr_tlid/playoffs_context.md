## 5. Patch in effect and notable hero changes

### Takeaway
mlbbhub lists Patch 2.2.16a as the current patch (released 14 Oct 2026) and it was already live well before the 4 Nov playoffs; the previous patch 2.2.16 (16 Sep) carried the big changes. Official confirmation that the playoff server runs 2.2.16a was not found (UNVERIFIED).

### Cited Findings
- Patch 2.2.16a, release date 14 Oct 2026, six hero changes: Aulus nerf (less sustain, keeps early-mid burst), Hirara nerf (skills used less often; full-combo burst slightly higher mid-late), Eudora nerf (ultimate slightly less damage at all levels), Rafaela nerf (S1 and S2 longer cooldowns at higher levels), Kalea buff (more solo-kill potential), Khufra adjustment (no description given). No item, emblem or battlefield changes listed — [mlbbhub 2.2.16a](https://mlbbhub.com/patch-notes/original/2.2.16a)
- mlbbhub's playoffs page footer states "Patch: 2.2.16a" as of 6 Nov 2026 — [mlbbhub playoffs](https://mlbbhub.com/mpl/id/playoffs)
- Patch 2.2.16, release 16 Sep 2026 (Season 42 "Starward Decade"), 25 hero changes. Reworks: Masha (sustained-combat Berserker), Bruno. Buffs: Aulus, Argus, Aldous, Lukas, Classic Akai, Kalea, Cici, Alpha, Kagura, Edith, Karina, Kaja. Nerfs: Classic Zilong, Melissa (shorter/weaker S1 attack-speed, less Muddle damage), Miya, Hanabi, Yi Sun-shin, Paquito (slightly less early-mid burst). Adjustments: Odette, Sun, Marcel (better synergy but reduced early damage/cast frequency; can interrupt location-targeted skills), Kadita, Hylos. Also: jungle camp base gold cut 13% (Horned Lizard, Fire Beetle, Lava Golem), team bounty deficit threshold 2,000 to 1,500, minion gold growth slightly reduced; map changes from 28 Sep (Revealing Wisps, Healing Turtle replacing the shield, Golden Turret); Enhanced Basic Attacks from hero skills can hit turrets; "Lowest HP" targeting becomes "Lowest Effective HP" — [mlbbhub 2.2.16](https://mlbbhub.com/patch-notes/original/2.2.16)

### Inferences
- Draft relevance: the 2.2.16a nerfs hit heroes that are heavily drafted (Hirara 94 picks / 85 bans, Aulus 63 picks, Eudora 51 picks); win-rate figures in section 6 mix pre- and post-patch games (see below), so they may overstate those heroes' current strength.
- Paquito (nerfed in 2.2.16) is still the most-picked hero (98) with a below-50% win rate (44%) and 73 bans; BTR and TLID both used him in the sampled H2H games.
- Kalea was buffed in both 2.2.16 and 2.2.16a; BTR's Finn played Kalea in match 1080 (19 Sep, after 2.2.16).

### Gaps
- Whether the MPL ID playoff server was on 2.2.16a (official tournament patch): UNVERIFIED. mlbbhub labels the same "2.2.16a" on 12 and 19 Sep match pages, which is impossible given the 14 Oct release, so that tag is a site-wide label rather than per-match data.
- Khufra's adjustment details: not described in the source.
- Patch-note source is a third-party site (mlbbhub); official Moonton notes and Liquipedia's Patch 2.2.16 page could not be fetched.
- Whether a later patch (e.g., a "b" revision) landed between 14 Oct and 7 Nov: not seen, but not ruled out.
