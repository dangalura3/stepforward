# Wolf (Caisam "Wolf" Nopueto) and the claim he "predicted 100% of the drafts"

Research date: 2026-11-07. About 27 tool calls. Most search results were generic. No search found a documented draft prediction by Wolf that could be checked against a result.

## Are there documented instances where Wolf predicted a draft (bans and picks) and got it right?

### Takeaway
I found no documented instance (article, tweet, clip description, Reddit thread) with a date, a match, a specific predicted draft, and a verified outcome. The "100%" claim is unsupported by anything I could reach. At best there is a general reputation for good draft analysis, plus a 2024 article saying the community expects "accurate draft analytics and game predictions" from him.

### Cited Findings
- No instance found. Searches combining Wolf with "predicted the draft", "called the draft", "draft prediction" and "100%", plus MPL, MSC and M-series, returned only generic Wolf coverage (draft simulator, coaching moves, pay disputes) and unrelated "Danny Wolf NBA draft" pages. None had a Wolf-specific, dated, checkable draft call.
- The nearest reputation evidence comes from an Ungeek interview with TNC head coach Wolf (Aug 2024). The article says the community expects his "accurate draft analytics and game predictions" to help him as a coach. That is the writer's framing of community expectation, not a measured record, and Wolf does not explain a drafting method in it. — [Ungeek, TNC Coach Wolf shares his coaching journey](https://ungeek.ph/2024/08/tnc-coach-wolf-shares-his-coaching-journey-his-visions-and-goals)
- In a Reddit AMA (r/MobileLegendsGame, Dec 21, 2023), caster Reptar was asked about rivals to Wolf, Midnight or himself at predicting drafts. He said he is not especially strong at it himself. He said "For Wolf and Midnight, since they have coaching experience, it's natural for them." He named Ko Lius (Indonesia) as good at predicting drafts, especially surprise picks. This is a peer's opinion, not a tally. The thread's page also includes a question about how often Wolf researches teams, with no answer shown. — [Reddit AMA mirror](https://cal1.lr.ggtyler.dev/r/MobileLegendsGame/s/Ih3gLiiPlO)
- Scoreline.ph (2024) calls him "the one and only King of MLBB Analysis". It credits his graphic explainers and a self-developed mobile app. It gives no prediction record, and the title is the article's own framing. — [Scoreline.ph](https://scoreline.ph/news/wolf-coaches-revamped-tnc-pro-team-s14-lineup)
- Spin.ph (Nov 2024) reports a dispute in which Indonesian outlet RevivalTV claimed Wolf criticized Team Liquid ID's draft and said they did not understand the meta. Wolf denied it and said he had praised their draft. He called their drafting "unpredictable" on his MPL ID livestream. This is relevant to the BTR vs TLID question because it is Wolf's own characterization of TLID drafting. It is not a prediction record. — [Spin.ph](https://www.spin.ph/esports/wolf-calls-out-indonesian-media-for-spreading-misinformation-a4858-20241106)
- A similar-looking but unrelated item: Omega coach Pakbet said his team "predicted" Blacklist International's draft in MPL PH (article Sept 12, 2022). The method was familiarity with Blacklist's unchanging draft pattern. Wolf is not mentioned. Do not attribute this to Wolf. — [GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/sports/othersports/844573/omega-s-coach-pakbet-reveals-they-predicted-blacklist-s-draft/story)
- Wolf's published predictions that I could read were outcome predictions, not draft predictions. For MPLI 2020 (article dated Nov 26, 2020) he named Bren Esports as one to watch, expected Aura PH and ONIC PH to surprise because they were new to the event, and called RRQ "very scary". — [ONE Esports](https://www.oneesports.gg/mobile-legends/gerry-eka-momochan-and-wolf-reveal-their-predictions-for-mpli/)

### Inferences
- "Predicted 100% of the drafts multiple times" most likely comes from fan exaggeration or from a few viral live-broadcast moments that I could not find. The claim may also be partly confused with other people's stories (see the Pakbet item).
- If he made such calls live (broadcast panels, streams, X posts), they would sit in VODs and tweets. Those were not reachable here, so absence of evidence here is not proof they never happened.

## What method or reasoning does Wolf describe (including the draft simulator)?

### Takeaway
The only concrete, sourced "method" is the 2023 draft simulator: pick heroes, see counters, and view team-composition ratings. The article does not say how the ratings are computed. Wolf has said little in readable sources about a prediction method (hero pools, signature heroes, ban priorities). The closest are general draft-reading comments from 2021.

### Cited Findings
- The draft simulator (Spin.ph, May 26, 2023): Wolf published a video about it a few weeks earlier. The interface lists all heroes. Selecting a hero shows its potential counters. As heroes are added to a team's pool, graphs rate the draft on teamfights, burst damage, early-to-mid game spike, wave clear and tower pushing. Examples in the article: ECHO PH (Fredrinn, Claude, Faramis, Benedetta, Chou) scored teamfights 9/10, burst 5.4, early-mid spike 6.2, wave clear 7.2, tower pushing 5.2. Bren Esports (Hayabusa, Lolita, Karrie, Yve, Gloo) scored teamfights 7.4, burst 5, tower pushing 3.8. The article says the ratings' calculation and data source are not explained. It carries no direct Wolf quotes and says he told them the app was not public yet. — [Spin.ph](https://www.spin.ph/esports/mlbb-shoutcaster-wolf-showcases-his-draft-simulator-program-a4858-20230526)
- Spin.ph's 2025 ONIC article says the simulator was later used on the MPL broadcast, and notes his attention to detail and use of statistics. — [Spin.ph](https://www.spin.ph/esports/onic-indonesia-welcomes-wolf-as-the-team-s-data-analyst-a4858-20250124)
- In a 2021 interview, Wolf said Malaysia and Singapore "like pocket picks" and "love cheese strategies", such as awkward heroes in other roles. He described Indonesia as favoring assassins in core and jungle roles. He advised teams facing those regions to "study their team". He framed Blacklist's Captain's Draft choices as aiming to beat the team that eliminated them and to learn other regions' metas. These are regional-style heuristics, not a formal predictor. — [Ungeek, Full Interview, MPLI 2021](https://ungeek.ph/2021/11/full-interview-wolf-shares-the-biggest-stories-and-top-teams-of-mpli-2021)
- Wolf's analysis edge, in his words (as reported): new ideas, the number of matches watched, and composure ("I am the captain of my own mental"). — [Ungeek, 2024](https://ungeek.ph/2024/08/tnc-coach-wolf-shares-his-coaching-journey-his-visions-and-goals)
- Scoreline credits "graphic explainers" and a "self-developed mobile app" for context and analysis. — [Scoreline.ph](https://scoreline.ph/news/wolf-coaches-revamped-tnc-pro-team-s14-lineup)
- The Reddit AMA remark from Reptar attributes Wolf's draft-reading to coaching experience. — [Reddit AMA mirror](https://cal1.lr.ggtyler.dev/r/MobileLegendsGame/s/Ih3gLiiPlO)
- Roster-building method (not drafting): "We listed all the available players" and evaluated them one at a time on value and fit. — [ONE Esports](https://www.oneesports.gg/mobile-legends/how-wolf-built-tnc-roster-mpl-ph/)
- Career context: he moved from caster to TNC head coach (MPL PH S14, 2024), then joined ONIC as data analyst (Jan 2025). — [Spin.ph](https://www.spin.ph/esports/onic-indonesia-welcomes-wolf-as-the-team-s-data-analyst-a4858-20250124)

### Inferences
- A usable, non-fabricated model of his approach, as far as the sources show: (1) team and region draft tendencies and habits (pocket picks, role preferences, repeated draft patterns); (2) composition-strength scoring across dimensions like teamfight, burst, spike, wave clear and push; (3) counter lookups; (4) coaching and scrim familiarity.
- I found no source in which Wolf states that he uses side, patch meta, signature heroes or opponent ban habits as explicit inputs. The tool should not claim he does.

### Gaps
- No YouTube transcript of him explaining a prediction method was reachable. Mirko + Wolf videos might contain it, but I could not read them.
- The simulator's scoring formula is undisclosed in the sources.
- Whether the simulator is now public: not established. Search results also show an unrelated site (mlbbhub.com) with a "draft simulator", which should not be confused with Wolf's.
