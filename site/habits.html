Coach and analyst habits

How drafters think, what I could and could not confirm, and what the simulator does with it. Read the "how sure" column: most of this is a lead, not a proven rule.

In plain words. Coaches and analysts say drafting is not only "best hero wins". Teams build around players and heroes that are doing well ("hot"), and they sometimes change plan mid-series. I checked the part I could check (do teams repeat bans?). The part about hot heroes I could not check, so it is a switch you control.

3. Measured on real ban lists

Question: after a game in a series, does a team ban the same heroes again? Data: 238 MPL ID and 130 MPL MY team-games from game 2 onward in a series.

LeagueSame bans after a winafter a losstwo random games of the same team
MPL ID S1846%38%18%
MPL MY S1842%36%19%

So teams repeat, and a bit more after a win. But does adding an extra "repeat" push help the model guess bans? Scored on each ban before it happened (average log-score, higher is better):

LeagueNo pushSmall push (+0.15 after win, −0.15 after loss)Big push
ID-3.232-3.227-3.391
MY-3.301-3.300-3.456

Result: no real gain (differences of 0.005 or less), and big pushes hurt. The model already counts games from the same series heavily, which captures this habit. So the push is left at zero.

What cannot be tested

"Hot hand" needs to know which player played which hero in each game and who was on a streak. Only BTR and TLID have pick data here, and the Malaysia and Philippines pages give bans only. So it stays a manual, unproven switch, off by default.
